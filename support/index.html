Frequently asked questions

Is Orant free?

Yes. Orant is free to download and use. It shows ads to help cover our costs.

Do I need an account?

No. Most of Orant works without an account. Creating an account lets you sync your progress and use community features such as prayer requests and groups.

My prayer reminders aren't showing up. What should I do?

Make sure notifications are turned on for Orant in your phone's settings. On Android, also allow "Alarms & reminders" for Orant so reminders arrive on time, and turn off battery optimization for the app if reminders are still late.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: open Orant, sign in, go to the Me tab, then Settings. Scroll to the end and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account and its data.

Can't open the app? Email info@babanorthlabs.com from the email address you signed up with and we'll delete it for you. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app. More details: orant.online/delete-account.

Is Orant available on iPhone?

Orant is coming soon to both Google Play and the App Store.
