About us

Baba North Labs is a Canadian software company. We design, build, and publish mobile apps for Android and iOS. We focus on calm, useful apps that respect people's time and privacy.

We handle everything in-house: product design, development, publishing on Google Play and the App Store, and customer support.

Our app: Orant

Orant is a Christian prayer and Bible app. It helps people build a daily habit of prayer and Bible reading.

  • Bible reader: read Scripture with a clean, distraction-free layout.
  • Guided prayer: prayers and reading plans for everyday life, with a prayer counter.
  • Quizzes: test and grow your Bible knowledge, book by book.
  • Journal: write down prayers and reflections, saved on your device.
  • Breathe: guided breathing to slow down and focus.
  • Reminders: gentle daily prayer reminders at the time you choose.

Orant is free and supported by ads.
